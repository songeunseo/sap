# Evidence for and against conditional-response-preserving DLM pruning

Date: 2026-09-18. Research assessment only; no new model evaluation or experiment.

## Hypothesis under assessment

At a fixed static weight budget, calibrating and selecting a sparse DLM to preserve both dense endpoints (A) and their paired context response (C) improves held-out denoising quality or downstream capability beyond a matched endpoint-only/ordinary fidelity objective. The proposed framework includes pair construction, a declared readout, exact-budget mask selection and hard-model verification. Framework scope by itself is not evidence for the hypothesis.

Separate three propositions: DLM context response is functionally meaningful; pruning distorts task-relevant parts of it; explicitly preserving it gives additional benefit over strong ordinary fidelity controls. Literature supports the first, while the final two remain much less established for the present model/mask family. The completed experiment is scalar gold-logodds A+C with nested gold reveals, not the proposed vector/matched-count framework.

## Supporting evidence

1. **Direct project screen, suggestive rather than confirmation.** Same native ranking family, exact50%, reused GSM8K mini100: Uniform54, A55, A+C61. ACvsA has9 rescued and3 regressed questions, exact paired p=.145996. This is the closest evidence that using the conditional term can change allocation usefully. It does not establish generalization, vector-readout benefit, or mediation by C. Existing calibration values also decrease: A-mask endpoint .665290/C .376338 versus AC-mask endpoint .658982/C .367000. Both components improve, so one cannot attribute the accuracy change uniquely to C reduction. Files: `experiments/dlm_context_response50/results.json`, `A/joint_distortion.json`, `AC/joint_distortion.json`.
2. **A functional DLM target, limited model scale.** [Induction in Both Directions](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.15893) observes a causal bidirectional induction circuit in small1–3-layer attention-only DLMs. Visible-context ablations with matched total mask count change induction performance; access to both sides matters. This motivates measuring how predictions use visible evidence. It does not demonstrate the same circuit in LLaDA8B, weight-pruning damage, or a successful allocator.
3. **Sensitivity preservation can help compressed diffusion models.** [2ndMatch](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.05398), Table4, reports CIFAR10 FID5.05 for noise prediction plus outputKD and4.58 with its second-order sensitivity objective (lower better). It supports the broad possibility that a response constraint adds to output matching. It is image diffusion with weight finetuning and J^T J matching, not our fixed-weight discrete-context allocation.

## Failure mechanisms and counterevidence

1. **C may add little beyond a strong A.** With e0,e1 the endpoint errors, A=.5(||e0||²+||e1||²), C=||e1-e0||². Let u=(e0+e1)/2,v=(e1-e0)/2. Exactly A+C=||u||²+5||v||² for lambda1, and C<=4A. C reweights context-dependent endpoint errors; it does not add independent target information. Under limited capacity this weighting might help or hurt. Exact endpoint preservation already preserves response. This is an algebraic limitation, not a proof that C is useless.
2. **The closest prior contains a negative ablation.** In 2ndMatch Table4, adding first-order Jacobian matching to outputKD gives FID5.14 versus5.05 without it; second-order matching helps. The authors discuss redundancy under their continuous-noise setting. Our finite discrete context differences are not that derivative or covered by its equivalence assumptions, but 'sensitivity matching helps' is not a sufficient justification for our particular C.
3. **Fidelity and task capability have diverged before.** At65% in a different project experiment, Global Minimax mean held-out KL was .645010 versus Role .660051, while frozen mini100 accuracy was16 versus24. This is a observed metric disagreement, not an AC experiment or a statistically established universal relationship. Lower calibration A+C therefore cannot serve as the sole success criterion. Source: `experiments/dlm_role_decision_audit/report.md`. KL is not NELBO.
4. **Content and progress are confounded in current pairs.** Current `make_pairs` reveals1–13 gold tokens and reduces mask count. [Subliminal Clocks](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.01774v2) shows progress-associated activation steering changes confidence and entropy; [Masks Can Be Distracting](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.21338) shows mask-count/context-locality effects. Thus current C may emphasize progress/confidence response as well as content use. This challenges causal attribution, not necessarily usefulness. Equal-count pairs are a control; they do not fix mask positions, perfectly isolate semantics, or justify removing progress behavior. The mask-distractor paper also reports no significant quantisation effect on these specific sensitivities in its tested conditions, so general compression amplification cannot be assumed.
5. **Calibration pairs may not cover deployed states.** The existing8 clean WikiText spans x10 masks yield80 pairs with gold reveals, rather than candidate-generated trajectories. A response preserved on correct evidence may not preserve behavior after mistaken commitments or on mathematical reasoning. This is a plausible distribution mismatch read from the actual setup, not measured failure of the new method. Repeated search on these8 documents can overfit.
6. **Readout and candidate-family bottlenecks.** Full-vector centered-logit MSE also spends capacity on changes among unlikely vocabulary items. It is unproven that this is better than the existing gold-focused scalar or KL. Frozen Wanda row order prevents correcting within-row ranking mistakes through allocation alone. These are identifiable risks, not observed causes of the current mini results.
7. **Algorithm quality/cost can fail independently.** A correct response criterion can be undermined by soft-hard mismatch, poor candidate proposals, or calibration cost. Existing naive local response extrapolation predicts AC1.338577 where actualAC1.025982, againstUniform1.202569; this invalidates that extrapolation, not all gradients.64 historical probe candidates required10,240 full forward calls. More elaborate search is not itself evidence that C adds value.

## Assessment

The strongest positive is the direct but small scalar mini screen; the most important scientific risk is redundancy with endpoint fidelity plus failure to align with held-out quality. The literature makes the question meaningful but does not yet validate a static LLaDA mask allocation based on C. Historical cached Uniform62 uses a different mask construction and prevents describing61 as overall best; matched nativeUniform54 remains the relevant allocation-family control. FullAC evaluation was stopped and AC NELBO has not been measured. No numeric success probability is defensible.

## Evidence that would change the assessment

Hold solver, hard-mask family, exact budget, pair endpoints, coefficient/scaling protocol and measured compute fixed. Compare vector endpoint-only, true paired A+C, and an appropriately matched broken-pair control; include a strong full-vocabulary fidelity control. Pair nulls must preserve usable query/output alignment and endpoint marginals. Different objectives must be compared on shared external quality metrics, not by their incomparable training-loss values. Use disjoint document splits; keep mini100 development-only. An additional matched-count condition can help identify what kind of response matters, without replacing realistic reveal states by assumption.

Support increases if true pairs improve independent NELBO/quality or match it at lower measured calibration cost, with an advantage over the matched controls. If C falls but held-out metrics do not improve, the presumed relevance of the response weighting weakens. If broken pairs perform similarly, genuine pair correspondence has no demonstrated added value. If only one solver works, investigate its search/candidate interaction before claiming framework-wide applicability. None of these tests is launched by this note.
