# Full GSM8K natural/cross-chain C control — final result review

## Fixed setup and outcome

Four 50%-sparse models, A-only, Natural-Multi (A+C natural), Cross-Multi (A+C cross), and Cross-Matched (A+β C cross, β=0.9323199077974966), were each freshly evaluated on all 1,319 GSM8K questions. The prespecified primary stratum is the remaining 1,119 after the previously inspected 200.

| Arm | Full 1,319 | Inspected 200 | Primary 1,119 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A-only | 764 (57.92%) | 118 (59.0%) | 646 (57.73%) |
| Natural-Multi | 758 (57.47%) | 122 (61.0%) | 636 (56.84%) |
| Cross-Multi | 757 (57.39%) | 120 (60.0%) | 637 (56.93%) |
| Cross-Matched | 755 (57.24%) | 121 (60.5%) | 634 (56.66%) |

Primary paired comparisons, Natural-Multi minus control; exact McNemar with Holm correction across the three prespecified comparisons; percentile paired bootstrap 10,000 draws, seed 20260927. Intervals are unadjusted.

| Comparison | Net questions | Gain/loss | Exact p | Holm p | 95% CI, percentage points |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Natural − A | −10 | 64/74 | 0.44372 | 1 | [−2.949, 1.162] |
| Natural − Cross | −1 | 74/75 | 1 | 1 | [−2.234, 1.966] |
| Natural − Cross-Matched | +2 | 67/65 | 0.93068 | 1 | [−1.787, 2.145] |

Result: None of the three prespecified primary advantages is established. A-only has the highest observed full and primary scores. This does not prove the four methods are equivalent.

## Interpretation and record status

The fixed-bank, fixed-allocation experiment does not support a GSM8K benefit from adding Natural C to A, nor a task benefit from preserving same-chain links over the cross-chain controls. This is a result for these masks, objective weights, model, bank, and evaluation protocol. It neither rules out other C designs nor identifies a layer mechanism. The inspected 200 had favored Natural over A by four questions, whereas the remaining 1,119 favored A by ten, which is why the predeclared primary stratum matters.

The pipeline report is `experiments/dlm_crosschain_control50/output/REPORT.md`; all raw data and paired statistics are in the adjacent `report.json` and `gsm8k/`. This supplemental review adds prior-response reproduction, error categories, and the archived first-attempt cost. Earlier implementation audit findings (stale config.plan metadata, validate receipt hazard, duplicate filename loader gap) remain documented in `REPORT.md` in this research directory. They did not manifest as duplicate or invalid current results.
